# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our promise

We want this project usable for anybody: staff records and leave dates are
sensitive enough that the people building the software around them have to be
straight with each other. Contributors are expected to keep the discussion about
the code.

## What we look for

Behaviour that helps:

- explaining a position once, with the reason and the file, instead of repeating
  it louder;
- reviewing a change by what it does to the data and to the person who has to
  operate it at eight in the morning;
- saying "I do not know this part" early, and asking;
- accepting that a maintainer can decide, and that a decision can be revisited
  with new evidence.

Behaviour we do not want:

- personal attacks, patronising tone, or gatekeeping by credentials;
- pushing a change by volume — repeated re-openings, mass mentions;
- publishing somebody else's data, real or scraped, in an issue, a test fixture
  or a screenshot;
- using the issue tracker as advertising.

## Where it applies

In the repository and everywhere you represent it: issues, pull requests,
discussions, release notes, and any mirror of this project. It also applies
outside those spaces when a conflict about this project follows you in.

## Reporting

Write to the maintainers through the private channel in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), or open an issue that says only "code of conduct
concern — please contact the maintainers" and wait for a reply before giving
detail. Complaints about a maintainer can go to any other maintainer.

Expect a first reply within a week or so; there is no committee and no paid
office behind this. Possible outcomes are an explanation, a request to withdraw
or edit a comment, a temporary ban from the repository's spaces, or no action
with the reason stated.
